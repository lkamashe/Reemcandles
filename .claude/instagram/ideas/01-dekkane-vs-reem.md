# Idea 01 — «شمعة الدكانة» vs reem (comparison story, 2 × 15 s)

Status: idea + image prompts sent to Ali. Video prompts come after Ali sends the images.

## The idea

A brother lights a cheap shop candle in the family living room; it gives black
smoke. His sister walks in with the reem bag, shows him that every reem candle
has its own ID number under the base, lights it: a clean warm flame and a scent
from Grasse. The cheap candle "retires".

True brand facts used: engraved ID number under every candle, fragrance oils
from Grasse, the drawer box and bag. Black smoke only on the non-reem candle.

## Dialogue (Lebanese)

Part 1
- Sister (enters, sees the smoke): «شو عم تشوي؟!»
- Brother (proud): «شمعة! جبتها من الدكانة.»
- Sister (puts the bag on the table): «طفيها. جبتلك شي أحلى.»
- Brother (drawer opens): «شو هيدا؟»

Part 2
- Sister (shows the base): «كل شمعة إلها رقمها.»
- Sister (lights it): «هيدي من ريم.»
- Brother (breathes in): «ولك شو هالريحة!»
- Sister: «ريحتها جاية من غراس.»
- Brother (pushes the cheap candle aside): «خلص، هيدي تقاعدت.»

On-screen hook text (editing): «مش كل شمعة شمعة»
End card: logo · قريباً · آخر تشرين الثاني · @reemcandles.co
