# Idea 01 — «شمعة الدكانة» vs reem (comparison story, 2 × 15 s)

Status: idea + image prompts sent to Ali. Video prompts come after Ali sends the images.

## The idea

A brother lights a cheap shop candle in the family living room; it gives black
smoke. His sister walks in with the reem bag, shows him that every reem candle
has its own ID number under the base, lights it: a clean warm flame and a scent
from Grasse. The cheap candle "retires".

True brand facts used: engraved ID number under every candle, fragrance oils
from Grasse, the drawer box and bag. Black smoke only on the non-reem candle.

## Dialogue (Lebanese)

Part 1
- Sister (enters, sees the smoke): «شو عم تشوي؟!»
- Brother (proud): «شمعة! جبتها من الدكانة.»
- Sister (puts the bag on the table): «طفيها. جبتلك شي أحلى.»
- Brother (drawer opens): «شو هيدا؟»

Part 2
- Sister (shows the base): «كل شمعة إلها رقمها.»
- Sister (lights it): «هيدي من ريم.»
- Brother (breathes in): «ولك شو هالريحة!»
- Sister: «ريحتها جاية من غراس.»
- Brother (pushes the cheap candle aside): «خلص، هيدي تقاعدت.»

On-screen hook text (editing): «مش كل شمعة شمعة»
End card: logo · قريباً · آخر تشرين الثاني · @reemcandles.co

## IMAGE PROMPT A — SISTER BOARD

Create one tall vertical 3:4 "CHARACTER CONTINUITY BOARD" image in the style of a professional film character design sheet: a clean light grey background, thin divider lines, bold clean sans serif headings, and photorealistic studio photographs of ONE single young woman shown in many views. She has exactly the same face, hair and clothes in every panel. Photorealistic, natural skin with real texture, no retouching look, no text inside the photos.

THE WOMAN: a Lebanese woman about 27 years old, elegant, confident and warm, with a light teasing sense of humor. Long straight dark brown hair worn down past the shoulders, dark brown eyes, olive skin, natural light makeup. Outfit: a camel wool long coat, open, over a fitted black knit top, black straight trousers, black ankle boots, small gold hoop earrings, a thin gold watch. Nothing in her hands.

LAYOUT OF THE BOARD, top to bottom:
Title bar: "CHARACTER CONTINUITY BOARD" in English on the left and "لوحة استمرارية الشخصية" in Arabic on the right.
Section 1, large panel with four full body views side by side: FRONT, 3/4 VIEW, SIDE, BACK. Relaxed natural standing pose, arms down at her sides, no stiff T pose. Short label under each in English and Arabic.
Section 2, on the right: CLOSE UP: a large face portrait, a coat and top detail, and an earring and watch detail.
Section 3: EXPRESSIONS, eight head and shoulder photos in a row: neutral, shocked, teasing smile, unimpressed, confident, proud, delighted, laughing. The mouth clearly visible in each.
Section 4: POSES AND BODY LANGUAGE: standing naturally, walking in, waving her hand in front of her face, carrying a paper gift bag by its handles in one hand, holding a small object up toward the camera with two hands, holding a lit wooden match.
Section 5: COLOURS AND MATERIALS: round color swatches (camel, black, dark brown hair, gold, olive skin) and material swatches (wool coat, knit top, straight hair, gold metal, leather boots). Do not write any color codes or numbers on the board.
Keep all written labels short, exactly spelled, and clearly readable in English and in Arabic. No gibberish text, no extra words.

## IMAGE PROMPT B — CHEAP CANDLE BOARD (the "dekkane" candle, NOT reem)

Create one tall vertical 3:4 "CANDLE CONTINUITY BOARD" image in the style of a professional product design sheet: a clean light grey background, thin divider lines, bold clean sans serif headings, and photorealistic photographs of ONE single cheap candle shown in many views and states. Exactly the same candle in every panel. No text, no label, no logo and no brand anywhere on the candle or the jar.

THE CANDLE: a cheap, plain, glossy bright red paraffin candle poured in a simple thin clear glass jar, about 8 centimeters tall and 7 centimeters wide. Uneven surface, a few air bubbles, a crooked thick wick. It looks cheap and ordinary, nothing elegant, no gold, no decoration.

LAYOUT OF THE BOARD, top to bottom:
Title bar: "CANDLE CONTINUITY BOARD" in English on the left and "لوحة استمرارية الشمعة" in Arabic on the right.
Section 1: FULL TURNAROUND: FRONT, 3/4 VIEW, SIDE, TOP VIEW, all unlit. Short label under each in English and Arabic.
Section 2: CLOSE UP: the crooked wick, the bubbly red wax surface, the thin glass rim.
Section 3: FUNCTION AND STATES, six panels: unlit; just lit with a tall unsteady orange flame; burning with thick black sooty smoke curling up from the flame; black soot marks on the inside of the glass; blown out with a thick dark smoke trail rising; burnt out with a sunken uneven pool.
Section 4: PROPORTIONS AND SCALE: next to a human hand for size, a low angle shot, a top view.
Section 5: COLOURS AND MATERIALS: round color swatches (bright red, clear glass, black soot, orange flame) and material swatches (glossy paraffin, thin glass, soot, crooked wick). Do not write any color codes or numbers on the board.
Keep all written labels short, exactly spelled, and clearly readable in English and in Arabic. No gibberish text, no extra words.
