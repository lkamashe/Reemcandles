# Prompt examples that worked (from Ali) — bedroom story reel

These are Ali's real prompts for MiniMax H3 (video) and for the continuity
boards (images). They are a working BASELINE, not a fixed template: keep the
level of detail, and improve the structure wherever it helps (Ali's words).
Do NOT reuse the story itself: each new idea must be different.

Improvements to apply on top of the baseline:
- Hook first: the strongest moment or line lands in the first 2 seconds of
  part 1 (no silent build-up before it).
- Fewer, longer beats: about 4 to 6 beats per 15 s clip, each spoken line with
  enough time for clean lip sync.
- Describe wanted states positively ("a clean steady flame", "the match is
  lowered out of frame still burning") instead of only naming what to avoid;
  naming an object (smoke) can pull it into the frame. Keep the short STRICT
  RULES recap at the end.
- Pre-flight checklist before handing any prompt to Ali (generation costs
  money): brand.md claims (no smell removal, no clean air), no smoke anywhere
  including references, exact product shape, box low/flat/drawer/logo on top,
  candle lit/unlit state per second, every spoken line in Lebanese with no
  tashkeel needed on screen, end frame defined, room left for on-screen text.

Known issues to avoid next time (they break brand.md rule 7 / rule 6):
- The story and the light shift ("stuffy, hazy air" -> "air looks clean and
  clear", "clean airy feeling" in the room board) imply the candle removes
  smells or cleans the air. Show a change of mood and warmth, not cleaner air.
- "She shakes out the match" can generate a smoke wisp. Keep the match moving
  out of frame instead.

## Video prompt structure (one prompt per 15 s clip)

1. Opening line: duration, 9:16, photorealistic, 24 fps; dialogue rules (exact
   Lebanese lines only, no narrator, no music, no on-screen text).
2. REFERENCES: number every image and say exactly what it locks
   (face, clothes, room, desk, box views, candle). Part 2 starts from the END
   FRAME of part 1 as Image 1.
3. START (part 1 only): exact opening state.
4. CHARACTERS: age, personality, voice; same face, hair, clothes every frame.
5. BOX SHAPE AND SIZE + BOX DETAILS: low, flat, 15x15x8 cm, drawer, no lid,
   logo on TOP only, "Made with love" + pull tab on the front, logo never
   changes.
6. CANDLE: exact shape from brand.md, what it is NOT, lit/unlit state per second.
7. SHOT BY SHOT: second ranges, who does what with which hand, each line of
   dialogue in quotes with its tone, silence where needed.
8. END FRAME: exact last frame (becomes the first frame of the next part).
9. CAMERA AND FRAMING, LIGHTING, SOUND (no music; music added in editing).
10. STRICT RULES: recap of every must / never.

## Board prompt structure (one per character, product or location)

Tall vertical 3:4 "... CONTINUITY BOARD", light grey background, bilingual
title bar, sections: FULL TURNAROUND (front, 3/4, side, back), CLOSE UP,
EXPRESSIONS or LIGHTING AND STATES, POSES or PROPORTIONS AND SCALE, COLOURS AND
MATERIALS. No colour codes or numbers written on the board, short exactly
spelled labels in English and Arabic, no text inside the photos, no smoke.

## VIDEO PART 1 (0–15 s)

Create a 15 second vertical 9:16 cinematic video, photorealistic, natural motion, 24 fps. Two characters speak in Lebanese Arabic, with clear natural voices and accurate lip sync. Use ONLY the exact spoken lines written below in quotes, word for word, in Lebanese Arabic dialect. Do not translate them, do not add any other words, no narrator, no music, no text or subtitles on screen.

REFERENCES: Image 1 is the mother: use her exact face, hair and clothes, the cream cardigan. Image 2 is the son: use his exact face, hair, headset and clothes. Image 3 is the exact room, a dark blue teenage bedroom at night: use the same walls, the same desk, the same chair, the same door with the gold door handle, the same lamp and the same decor. Image 4 is the exact desk close up with the pizza box and the glass: keep those objects on the desk. Images 5, 6 and 7 show the exact gift box from the three quarter view, from the front with the pull tab, and from above with the logo. Use the box exactly as shown, same shape, same proportions, same color, same logo. Image 8 shows the exact rose candle. Use the candle exactly as shown.

START: The video starts in the room of Image 3 with the son sitting at the desk as in Image 2, wearing the headset, playing on the computer. The door is closed. The room is dim, cool blue, stuffy and heavy, with a slightly hazy dull air and only the blue glow of the screen and a weak lamp.

CHARACTERS: The mother is a warm Lebanese woman in her forties, caring and a little strict, with a warm voice. The son is a Lebanese young man around 18 to 20, relaxed and playful, with a young male voice. Both keep the same face, hair and clothes in every frame. Natural facial expressions, mouths clearly visible when they speak.

BOX SHAPE AND SIZE, VERY IMPORTANT: The gift box is a LOW, FLAT, wide box, like a small flat jewelry or gift drawer box. It is almost square from above, about 15 centimeters wide, 15 centimeters deep and only about 8 centimeters tall, small enough to carry in one hand. It ALWAYS lies flat. It NEVER stands upright, it is NEVER tall, it NEVER grows bigger. It is matte dark olive with NO lid: it is a sleeve with a drawer that slides out horizontally from the front.

BOX DETAILS: On the flat TOP face there is a thin gold foil rectangular border, and inside it a gold circular emblem with a seated gazelle between two laurel branches, the word reem in gold script under the gazelle, and the small words HAND MADE CANDLES under it, exactly as in Images 5, 6 and 7. The emblem is on the TOP face only. On the FRONT face is the drawer front with the gold script words Made with love and one olive grosgrain pull tab loop in the center. THE LOGO MUST STAY EXACTLY AS IT IS: same circle, same gazelle, same laurel branches, same lettering, same gold color, same size and position on the top face. It must never change, never morph, never be redrawn, never be mirrored, never move to another side, and never get extra or missing letters in any frame.

CANDLE: The rose candle is an ivory cone shaped tree candle, about 12 centimeters tall and 6 centimeters wide at the base, covered from the base to the pointed top with overlapping tiers of sculpted three dimensional roses and small leaves, with gold leaf accents, exactly as in Image 8. It is NOT a pillar, NOT a cylinder, and it has NO large gold leaf relief shapes. A plain cotton wick is at the very top tip. The candle is UNLIT for the whole video and stays inside the drawer.

SHOT BY SHOT, with the dialogue (one continuous take, no cuts):
0 to 1.5 seconds: The son plays on the computer, head moving slightly, absorbed. The door behind him is closed. Only quiet game sounds and room tone. Nobody speaks.
1.5 to 3.2 seconds: The door opens and the mother steps inside the room. She is completely silent until she is fully inside: no voice is heard before the door opens or before she has stepped in. Once inside she stops, wrinkles her nose and waves her hand in front of her face, surprised and annoyed, as if the air is heavy and stuffy.
3.2 to 4.6 seconds: She says in Lebanese Arabic, with a surprised, annoyed tone, not smiling: "يا ساتر! شو هالريحة هاي؟"
4.6 to 5.8 seconds: She looks at the pizza box on the desk, angry and disapproving, and says in Lebanese Arabic: "بيتزا بغرفة النوم؟!"
5.8 to 7.3 seconds: The son pulls one side of the headset off his ear, turns to her with an innocent, playful look, and says in Lebanese Arabic: "أي ريحة يا ماما؟ ما في شي!"
7.3 to 8.6 seconds: The mother looks at her son, crosses her arms with a small determined look, and says in Lebanese Arabic: "استنى شوي." She turns and walks out of the room, and the door closes behind her.
8.6 to 10.6 seconds: The mother is gone and the son is alone. The camera pushes in slowly toward the son while he sits worried and uneasy, glancing at the closed door, the headset hanging around his neck. Silence, only room tone.
10.6 to 12 seconds: Two seconds after she left, the door opens again. The mother walks in carrying the small closed flat olive box in one hand, held flat, the drawer front with the words Made with love facing forward. She walks straight to the desk.
12 to 12.8 seconds: She sets the box down flat on the desk next to the computer, beside the pizza box and the glass, and says in Lebanese Arabic, with a warm, confident smile: "تعا شوف."
12.8 to 15 seconds: She grips the olive grosgrain pull tab and slides the drawer out slowly toward the camera, about 10 centimeters. Inside the open drawer the unlit rose candle lies on soft ivory paper filling, pointed top toward the right. The son leans in with interest and asks in Lebanese Arabic, curious: "ماما، شو هاي؟" The gold emblem on the top of the box stays visible and upright. The candle stays in the drawer and is not taken out yet.

END FRAME: The low flat olive box lying open on the desk beside the pizza box, the drawer pulled out about 10 centimeters toward the camera, the unlit rose cone candle lying on ivory paper inside the drawer, the mother's hand still on the pull tab, the son leaning in curious, the gold emblem visible on the top of the box. The room is still dim and cool blue. This exact frame is the starting frame of the next part.

CAMERA AND FRAMING: Vertical 9:16, stable tripod, medium shot from the front of the room showing the desk, the son and the door, with only a very slow gentle push in toward the son while he is alone in the room. The mother never fills the frame and there is no close-up of her. From the very first frame the son is already seated at the desk. For the box and candle moments the camera is slightly higher, looking down at about 30 degrees at the desk. Both faces stay clearly visible. Space at the top of the frame for text added later.

LIGHTING: Dim, cool, dark blue, with the screen glow on the son's face and a weak lamp. Cool and heavy mood. The candle is unlit, so there is no warm flame light yet.

SOUND: Natural sound plus the spoken lines only: faint game sounds, mouse clicks, the door handle and door opening and closing, footsteps, the soft thud of the box on the desk, the slide of the drawer, the rustle of the paper filling, a quiet room tone. Voices clear and natural, in Lebanese Arabic. No music.

STRICT RULES: Speak only the four mother lines and the two son lines written above, exactly as written, in Lebanese Arabic. The mother says nothing before she has stepped into the room. The box is always low, flat and lying down, never tall and never upright, always small. Never change or redraw the logo or any letter on the box; the emblem stays on the top face only. The candle is always the ivory rose cone tree candle, never a pillar, never gold relief, and it stays unlit. Do not show cigarettes, smoke, vaping or any drugs anywhere. The room, the characters and the clothes never change. No text on screen, no subtitles, no extra logos, no extra people, no animals. Keep all motion realistic, nothing floating, nothing morphing.

## VIDEO PART 2 (15–30 s)

Create a 15 second vertical 9:16 cinematic video, photorealistic, natural motion, 24 fps. Two characters speak in Lebanese Arabic, with clear natural voices and accurate lip sync. Use ONLY the exact spoken lines written below in quotes, word for word, in Lebanese Arabic dialect. Do not translate them, do not add any other words, no narrator, no music, no text or subtitles on screen.

Start exactly from this frame. Image 2 is the mother: keep her exact face, hair and cream cardigan. Image 3 is the son: keep his exact face, hair and clothes. Image 4 is the exact room. Image 5 is the exact desk close up. Images 6 and 7 show the exact gift box from the front with the pull tab and from above with the logo. Use the box exactly as shown. Image 8 shows the exact rose candle. Use the candle exactly as shown.

CHARACTERS: The mother is a warm Lebanese woman in her forties, caring, with a warm voice. The son is a Lebanese young man around 18 to 20, relaxed and playful, with a young male voice. Both keep the same face, hair and clothes in every frame. Natural expressions, mouths clearly visible when they speak.

BOX SHAPE AND SIZE, VERY IMPORTANT: The gift box is a LOW, FLAT, wide box, like a small flat jewelry or gift drawer box, almost square from above, about 15 centimeters wide, 15 centimeters deep and only about 8 centimeters tall. It ALWAYS lies flat on the desk, open with the drawer pulled out toward the camera. It NEVER stands upright, it is NEVER tall, it NEVER grows bigger. It is matte dark olive with no lid. On its flat TOP face there is a thin gold foil rectangular border and a gold circular emblem with a seated gazelle between two laurel branches, the word reem in gold script, and the small words HAND MADE CANDLES, exactly as in Image 7. The drawer front has the gold script words Made with love and the olive grosgrain pull tab. THE LOGO MUST STAY EXACTLY AS IT IS: same circle, same gazelle, same laurel branches, same lettering, same gold color, same position on the top face. It must never change, morph, be redrawn, be mirrored, move to another side, or get extra or missing letters in any frame.

CANDLE: The rose candle is an ivory cone shaped tree candle, about 12 centimeters tall and 6 centimeters wide at the base, covered with overlapping tiers of sculpted three dimensional roses and small leaves with gold leaf accents, exactly as in Image 8. It is NOT a pillar, NOT a cylinder, and has NO large gold leaf relief shapes. A plain cotton wick is at the very top tip. The candle is unlit at the start, is lifted out of the drawer by the mother, and is lit by the match at second 3.

SHOT BY SHOT, with the dialogue:
0 to 2.5 seconds: The mother lifts the unlit rose candle gently out of the open drawer and sets it standing on its base on the desk in front of the box, pointed top up, wick unlit. The box stays low and flat beside it with the gold emblem visible on its top. She takes a wooden match from her cardigan pocket, strikes it, and a small flame appears on the match. She says in Lebanese Arabic, with a warm confident smile: "هلق بتشوف." The son watches closely.
2.5 to 4 seconds: She touches the match flame to the cotton wick. The wick catches and a small flame appears, then grows to a steady soft flame. She shakes out the match. At the same moment the warm golden light of the flame spreads over the roses, the gold leaf, the box, the desk and both faces. From here the whole room slowly shifts from cool dark blue to warm golden amber, the air looks clean and clear, and the mood becomes cozy.
4 to 7 seconds: The mother closes her eyes, breathes in slowly through her nose with a peaceful smile and says in Lebanese Arabic, with delight: "الله! شو هالريحة الحلوة!" The son leans toward the candle, breathes in, his eyebrows rise, impressed. He does not speak in this part.
7 to 10.5 seconds: The mother opens her eyes, proud and smiling, and says in Lebanese Arabic: "من ريم. شمعة بتغيّر جو الغرفة كلها." The son looks around the room, now warm and glowing, and nods slowly, smiling.
10.5 to 12.5 seconds: The son looks at the room, then at the candle, pleasantly surprised and happy, and says in Lebanese Arabic: "غرفتي صارت أحلى!"
12.5 to 14.2 seconds: The son looks at his mother with a warm smile and says in Lebanese Arabic: "شكراً ماما." The mother answers softly in Lebanese Arabic, touching his shoulder: "الله يخليك."
14.2 to 15 seconds: The camera pushes in slowly on the lit rose candle with the open flat box beside it, the gold emblem visible on its top, the warm room softly blurred behind, the two figures soft in the background. No speech, only the soft flame.

END FRAME: The lit rose candle with a steady flame on the desk, the low flat open olive box beside it with the gold emblem on its top, the room warm and golden, soft and blurred behind.

CAMERA AND FRAMING: Vertical 9:16, stable tripod, the same medium shot angle as Image 1, looking slightly down at the desk, with a very slow gentle push in. The camera keeps a medium framing, the mother never fills the frame. Both faces stay visible and clear when they speak. Space at the top of the frame for text added later.

LIGHTING: Starts dim and cool blue, then the candle flame gives warm golden light that gradually fills the room into soft amber. Gentle highlights on the roses and the gold leaf, warm glow on the faces. The shift from cool blue to warm gold is smooth and gradual, never a sudden jump.

SOUND: Natural sound plus the spoken lines only: the strike and crackle of the match, the small flutter of the flame, a quiet room tone. Voices clear and natural, in Lebanese Arabic. No music.

STRICT RULES: Speak only the lines written above, exactly as written, in Lebanese Arabic. The box is always low, flat, small and lying down. Never change or redraw the logo or any letter on the box; the emblem stays on the top face only. The candle is always the ivory rose cone tree candle, never a pillar, never gold relief. It stays unlit until the match touches the wick at second 3. It always stands on the desk in front of or beside the box, never on top of the box, and it never covers the emblem. Do not show cigarettes, smoke, vaping or any drugs anywhere. The room, the characters and the clothes never change except for the warm light. No text on screen, no subtitles, no extra logos, no extra people, no animals. Keep all motion realistic, nothing floating, nothing morphing.

## BOARD: ROOM (the son's bedroom)

Create one tall vertical 3:4 "ROOM CONTINUITY BOARD" image in the style of a professional film production design sheet: a clean light grey background, thin divider lines, bold clean sans serif headings, and photorealistic photographs of ONE single room shown in many views. All photos must show exactly the same room with exactly the same furniture, same objects, same colors and same layout in every panel. Photorealistic, no people anywhere, no cigarettes, no smoke, no text inside the photos.

THE ROOM: a young man's bedroom in a Lebanese family apartment, about 4 by 4 meters, at night. Walls painted in deep dark navy blue, matte. Light grey wood floor with a dark rug in the center. In the middle of the back wall a large wooden desk with a gaming computer, a monitor with a cool blue screen glow, a keyboard, a mouse, and a dark gaming chair with blue accents. On the desk: an open pizza box with a few slices left, and a glass of juice. A single bed with dark grey bedding along the left wall. On the right wall a tall wooden cabinet with closed doors. A white interior door with a simple gold round door handle, on the right side of the front wall. One weak warm desk lamp, a single window with closed dark curtains, a few framed posters, a small bookshelf with game boxes. The air in the room looks heavy, dim and stuffy, cool blue, lived in, a bit messy.

LAYOUT OF THE BOARD, top to bottom:
Title bar: "ROOM CONTINUITY BOARD" in English on the left and "لوحة استمرارية الغرفة" in Arabic on the right.
Section 1, large panel with four views side by side: FRONT VIEW (from the door looking at the desk), 3/4 VIEW, SIDE VIEW, REAR VIEW (from the desk looking at the door). Under each a short label in English and Arabic.
Section 2, on the right: CLOSE UP panels: the desk with the monitor, the pizza box and the glass; the door and its gold handle; the cabinet.
Section 3: LIGHTING AND STATES, six small panels: dim stuffy blue night (main state), same room with the lamp only, same room with warm golden candle light spreading from the desk and a clean airy feeling, door open view, door closed view, daylight for reference.
Section 4: PROPORTIONS AND SCALE: a top down floor plan photo of the room, an entrance view from the door, a low angle shot from the desk, each showing the same furniture in the same place.
Section 5: COLOURS AND MATERIALS: round color swatches (navy wall, grey wood floor, dark wood desk, warm lamp yellow, screen blue) and material swatches (matte navy paint, wood, gaming chair fabric, bedding). Do not write any color codes or numbers on the board.
Keep all written labels short, exactly spelled, and clearly readable in English and in Arabic. No gibberish text, no extra words.
